# Tomo Island

🥈 2nd Place, Canada Life Track, Hack Western 2026

What if you could foresee the future? Welcome to Tomo Island; a video game that encourages people to save money, and see their island come to life by doing so. 

Here's how it works: Every time you put real money into savings, you log it at the island bank, and your island rewards you for showing up. Coins come from consistency, not the size of your deposit, so five dollars a day beats one giant transfer every time. Spend those coins in the shop on palm trees, lanterns, cottages and more, and slowly turn a little patch of sand into a place that's completely yours. Stuck on a money question? Maple, the island's fox, is always around to chat about TFSAs, budgeting, or that thing you really want to buy right now. Every day there's a new Daily Star, a hot-or-cold word game where each hidden star word unlocks a real, niche way to save. Team up with friends in a town, cheer each other on, and build something bigger together.

Built in 36 hours at Hack Western 13.

## Features
**Savings and Rewards**
- Daily savings log: connect a bank through Plaid to automatically log how much you saved each day. Coins are rewarded based on consistency (daily streak), not the amount
- Shop: coins buy items from the shop that players can use to decorate their island
- Goals: set long term savings goals to work towards
- History & future view: see your island projected 1, 10 and 20 years in the future

**Social**
- Towns: join a town with your friends to work together towards a shared finance goal. Send encouraging messages, complete tasks to build your project in the island.
- Daily star: a quick daily mini-game. Guess a word related to a finance tip.
- Character customization: change your avatar to look more like you!
- Sync devices: use a code to sync accounts across mobile & desktop
- Maple AI Chatbot: ask Maple finance questions or about life in general

## Built with
- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- Backend: Node.js and Express
- AI: Google Gemini API Key (Maple's chat and Daily Star hints)
- Banking: Plaid
- Database: Firebase/Firestore
