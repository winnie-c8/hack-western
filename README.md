# Tomo Island

What if you could foresee the future? Welcome to Tomo Island; a video game that encourages people to save money, and see their island come to life by doing so. 

Here's how it works: Every time you put real money into savings, you log it at the island bank, and your island rewards you for showing up. Coins come from consistency, not the size of your deposit, so five dollars a day beats one giant transfer every time. Spend those coins in the shop on palm trees, lanterns, cottages and more, and slowly turn a little patch of sand into a place that's completely yours. Stuck on a money question? Maple, the island's fox, is always around to chat about TFSAs, budgeting, or that thing you really want to buy right now. Every day there's a new Daily Star, a hot-or-cold word game where each hidden star word unlocks a real, niche way to save. Team up with friends in a town, cheer each other on, and build something bigger together.

## Features

- Record what players saved each day in their daily savings log, and the rewards go to their piggy bank, not the amount
- Connect a bank through Plaid by signing in inside Plaid's own window, so their bank password never reaches Tomo
- Players choose a single savings account to share, and the app only asks Plaid for savings accounts
- Tomo checks for new deposits into that account and can't move money, change anything at the bank or see spending
- The Plaid access key is held by the backend and never sent to the browser
- Players can skip the bank link and log by hand or disconnect any time, and the current version is a sandbox demo with fake banks and money
- Small daily actions earn rewards and build a streak that makes steady saving feel good
- Coins buy items from the shop that players place on their island and use to customize their character
- Players set savings goals and see their island projected 1, 10 and 20 years ahead, with growth labeled as an example, not a guarantee
- Friends build shared projects that count habits only, never dollar amounts, while the daily mini-game adds a quick, fun challenge
